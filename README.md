# Climate City — Explore the Vision

Concept masterplan, architectural image collection and interactive website for Mikoshel Limited's proposed Climate City in Nasarawa State, Nigeria.

## Deliverables

- `dist/index.html`: Climate City landing page adapted from the user-supplied Curevo v1.0 HTML template.
- `dist/explore.html`: labelled aerial masterplan and rotatable three-dimensional concept study.
- `dist/data.json`: destination titles, captions and image paths.
- `dist/climate-city-masterplan.pdf`: labelled concept masterplan and interior collection.
- `dist/assets/*.webp`: optimized architectural views.
- `prompts/`: exact prompts and metadata used for built-in image generation.
- `source-art/`: original generated images and brochure reference images, retained locally.

## Experience

Select a destination on the masterplan or in the directory. A camera animation opens its image journey. Scrolling advances through images with subtle pan, scale and crossfades; descriptions sit left on desktop and below on mobile. Keyboard controls, reduced-motion support, next/previous and Back to city are available. The 3D city uses procedural geometry and software perspective projection on Canvas; the high-detail aerial is a separately generated illustration.

This preview uses still-image animation, not rendered camera-flight videos. The user requested review of images and interactions before paid video production. No paid video generation was performed.

## Planning basis

The 1,200-hectare extent is supplied by the user. Layouts are conceptual, not surveyed, approved or engineering-validated. The train station, diplomatic village expansion, hotel waterfront and individual rooms are design proposals. The brochure's project remains proposed and pre-development. No claims of operational facilities, approved rail links or confirmed water conditions are made.

## Design provenance

Architectural material language comes from the supplied Climate City brochure: sandstone, terracotta screens, timber, shaded courtyards and planting. New artwork was generated using the built-in image tool. The website uses the user-supplied Curevo template; original template attribution is preserved in source comments where applicable. No template medical testimonials, doctors, treatment prices or stock clinic claims are repurposed as Climate City facts.

## Local development

Serve `dist/` with a local static web server. No dependency installation or build is required. The separate private Sites hosting configuration is not included in this GitHub repository.

Browser visual verification was unavailable because local browser access was disabled in Work settings. Source syntax, referenced assets and PDF rendering are checked separately.
